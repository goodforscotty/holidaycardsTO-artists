We’re a small collective of independent artists who come together each holiday season to create, promote and sell a curated collection of greeting cards.

How it started

The project began with a group of artist friends pooling their work and resources to help each other make a little more money during an expensive time of year. It has grown into a shared collection with professional production, an online shop, collective marketing and growing wholesale opportunities.

The idea is simple: combine our creativity, audiences, relationships and resources so everyone has the opportunity to sell more than they could working alone.

What you get

More than a place to list your cards.

The value of the collective is shared infrastructure, shared audiences and shared opportunity. We handle many of the things that are harder for an independent artist to build alone.

Participation

What we ask from artists

Participation means more than submitting artwork. Everyone should contribute to making the collective stronger, but that contribution can look different depending on your strengths, audience and capacity.

Bring strong work

Contribute artwork that adds something distinctive to the collection.

Promote the collection

Share launches, products and important sales moments with your own audience.

Help expand our reach

Introduce retail, wholesale or promotional opportunities when possible.

Participate

Stay engaged with important conversations and occasional group planning.

Meet agreed deadlines

Submit artwork and required materials in time for the group to stay on schedule.

Think collectively

Help build an opportunity that becomes stronger for everyone involved.

Nobody is expected to do everything. People can contribute in different ways. What matters is that everyone brings something meaningful to the effort.

Growing the collective

Why we’re adding new artists

We’re not interested in simply making the shop bigger. We’re looking for artists who bring something new creatively, introduce the collection to new audiences and want to help grow the opportunity with us.

Adding an artist should expand the opportunity for everyone, not simply add more designs competing for the same sales.

The practical stuff

The money part

We want everyone to understand exactly how the project works financially before deciding whether to participate.

Artist compensation

Artist earnings, production costs, wholesale pricing and payment details will be confirmed with invited artists before anyone commits.

Do I still own my artwork?

Yes. Participating artists remain the owners of their artwork.

Do I need to manage customer orders?

No. Orders are handled through the shared online store and fulfilment process.

How many designs do I need?

We’ll work with each invited artist to determine an appropriate starting assortment.

What files will I need to provide?

We’ll provide print templates, specifications and web-image requirements.

Do I have to attend lots of meetings?

No. We occasionally plan together, but the project is intended to remain practical and manageable.

Interested?

If you received an invitation, it means we already think your work could be a great addition. If this sounds like a good fit for you, register your interest and we’ll reach out to connect and share more details.
